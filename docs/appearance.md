# Relay appearance

Relay offers Light, Dark and System appearance. System follows your operating system and is the default.

Use the appearance icon beside the Relay wordmark to cycle through the three choices, or open Settings and choose Appearance. On a small screen, open the agent roster to reach the icon. Your selection is saved for this Relay installation; a validated browser preference cache supplies the initial appearance while Relay loads.

In Agent mode, you can also ask a connected bot: “Switch Relay to dark mode,” “Use light mode,” or “Follow my system appearance.” The bot uses the existing settings control. This changes appearance without editing Relay's source or asking you to approve a build.

The Quiet Radiance design uses warm neutral light surfaces, deep neutral dark surfaces, soft amber and teal light at the edges, and selective glass on navigation and floating controls. Conversations, code, forms and credential fields retain readable, solid surfaces.

Relay's chats, settings, dialogs, workspace, browser controls and terminal palette follow the chosen appearance. Changing the palette keeps an existing terminal session and its output in place. Visited websites, streamed page images, uploaded images and custom bot identities retain their own colors.

Relay respects reduced motion, reduced transparency and forced system colors. Appearance changes do not submit credentials or change account usage permissions.
